TAJ MAHAL

The Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the World, famous for its breathtaking beauty, magnificent architecture, and timeless symbol of love.

1. Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a magnificent white marble monument located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal.

📍 Location

Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India

🏛️ Built By

Shah Jahan

❤️ Built For

Mumtaz Mahal

📅 Completed

17th Century

🏗️ Material

White Marble

🌍 UNESCO

World Heritage Site

📜 History

The Taj Mahal was commissioned by Shah Jahan after the death of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Construction began in the 17th century and the monument became one of the greatest examples of Mughal architecture.

The monument was created as a symbol of love and remembrance. It attracts millions of visitors from around the world every year.

🏛️ Architecture

The Taj Mahal combines elements of Persian, Islamic and Indian architectural styles. Its main structure is made primarily of white marble and is decorated with beautiful carvings and precious stone inlay work.

The complex includes a large central dome, four minarets, beautiful gardens, a mosque and a reflecting pool.

✨ Special Features

🕌 Grand Dome

The large central dome is one of the most recognizable features of the Taj Mahal.

🗼 Four Minarets

Four elegant minarets surround the main structure and create perfect visual balance.

🌿 Beautiful Gardens

The monument is surrounded by beautifully designed Mughal-style gardens.

💎 Marble Decoration

Intricate carvings and colorful stone inlays decorate the walls of the monument.

💡 Interesting Facts

  • The Taj Mahal is located on the banks of the Yamuna River.
  • It is one of India's most famous tourist attractions.
  • The monument is famous for its beautiful white marble.
  • The Taj Mahal changes appearance under different lighting conditions.
  • It is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • It is considered one of the finest examples of Mughal architecture.

❤️ Why Is Taj Mahal Famous?

The Taj Mahal is famous throughout the world for its extraordinary beauty, detailed architecture and emotional story. It represents love, art and the remarkable architectural achievements of the Mughal era.
