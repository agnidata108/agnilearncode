CHRIST THE REDEEMER

Christ the Redeemer is one of the Seven Wonders of the World, famous for its magnificent statue, breathtaking views, and cultural importance to Brazil.

7. Christ the Redeemer

Christ the Redeemer is a famous statue of Jesus Christ located on Mount Corcovado in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. With its open arms overlooking the city, the monument has become one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world.

📍 Location

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

🏛️ Designed By

Heitor da Silva Costa

🗿 Statue

Jesus Christ

📅 Completed

1931

🏗️ Material

Concrete & Soapstone

⛰️ Location

Mount Corcovado

📜 History

The idea of building a large statue of Christ on Mount Corcovado developed in Brazil during the early 20th century. The project was eventually approved and construction began in the 1920s.

Christ the Redeemer was officially inaugurated in 1931. Since then, it has become an important symbol of Rio de Janeiro and Brazil.

🏛️ Architecture

The statue stands on the summit of Mount Corcovado and features Jesus Christ with his arms stretched wide. The structure was designed using reinforced concrete and covered with soapstone tiles.

Its enormous scale and carefully designed structure make it an impressive example of modern monument construction. The open-arm design represents peace and welcome.

✨ Special Features

🗿 Giant Statue

The monument is a massive statue of Jesus Christ with his arms spread wide over Rio de Janeiro.

⛰️ Mountain Location

The statue stands on Mount Corcovado and provides spectacular views of Rio de Janeiro.

🤲 Open Arms

The statue's open arms are widely seen as a symbol of peace, love and welcome.

🌎 World Landmark

Christ the Redeemer is one of Brazil's most famous landmarks and a major tourist attraction.

💡 Interesting Facts

  • Christ the Redeemer is located in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
  • The statue was completed in 1931.
  • It stands on Mount Corcovado overlooking the city.
  • The statue is made mainly of reinforced concrete and soapstone.
  • It is one of the most recognizable symbols of Brazil.
  • The monument is one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

⭐ Why Is Christ the Redeemer Famous?

Christ the Redeemer is famous for its enormous size, beautiful location and powerful symbolism. Standing above Rio de Janeiro with its arms open wide, the statue has become a global symbol of peace, faith and welcome.
